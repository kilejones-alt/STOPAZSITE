Case Study — California Ethnic Studies Curriculum

Examples drawn from California’s Ethnic Studies curriculum illustrate how antizionist narratives quietly enter educational spaces under the language of social justice and human rights. These statements are presented to students as objective descriptions of historical and geopolitical realities. Yet, they embed ideological claims that demonize Israel, erase Jewish indigeneity, and frame Zionism as an oppressive or racialized project.

When such claims appear unchallenged in curriculum materials, they shape young minds through repetition, authority bias, and emotional framing, often without students or educators realizing that they are encountering long-standing antizionist libels rather than neutral facts.

Can you identify the antizionist libels embedded in them?

  • “Palestinians live under an ongoing apartheid.”
  • “Gaza is an open-air prison, similar to the Warsaw ghetto.”
  • “Israel has committed genocide in Gaza.”
  • “The creation of Israel resulted in the displacement of three-quarters of a million Palestinians.”
  • “Palestinians in the West Bank have almost no rights at all under Israeli occupation, and the international consensus is that Israel is occupying Palestine.”
  • “Palestinian students’ life stories involve the harm caused to them and their families by the creation of Israel and ongoing occupation of Palestine, both of which deprived Palestinians like them and their relatives of the right to self-determination.”
  • “The Nakba (the dispossession of Palestinians caused by the establishment of the State of Israel), the occupation of the West Bank and blockade of Gaza, and the claims of numerous scholars and human rights organizations that Israel has committed war crimes in Gaza and the West Bank and genocide in Gaza.”

These are not neutral descriptions. The occupation, apartheid, genocide, and ethnic cleansing libels are ideological narratives designed to demonize and delegitimize Jewish self-determination. These are the markers of antizionism, an era that constructs a villain around Israel to portray it as a demon who violates the purity of human rights.

Stop Antizionism provides tools for educators, administrators, and communities to identify antizionism as it appears in learning materials. California’s Ethnic Studies curriculum offers clear case studies: language that presents Zionism as a racialized ideology, erases Jewish indigeneity, or portrays Jews as foreigners in their ancestral land.

Stop Antizionism helps educators recognize these libels, understand their origins, and respond with confidence. Stop Antizionism provides training, educational series, workshops, and curriculum consulting to help identify antizionism and most importantly, confront it by calling out the libels.

STOPAZ CERTIFICATE PROGRAM

The Stop Antizionism Certification Program offers structured, expert-led training designed to help individuals understand, analyze, and effectively respond to antizionism in today’s social, academic, and policy environments. Whether you are new to the topic or already engaged in advocacy, our two-track system provides a clear pathway from foundational knowledge to advanced leadership and strategic influence.

LEADERSHIP TRAINING

Register for STOP AZ Leadership Track: 3 sessions @ 90 min: Antizionism 101, Analysis, Application

Lead with Knowledge.  Designed for advocates, organizational leaders, policymakers, and professionals, this advanced program develops expertise in strategic communications, policy engagement, advocacy, leadership, and responding effectively to antizionist narratives across institutions, media, and public discourse.

EDUCATOR TRAINING

Register for STOP AZ Educator Track: 3 sessions @ 90 min/s: Antizionism 101, Analysis, Application

Teach the next generation. Designed for educators, trainers, community leaders, and professionals who want to educate others about antizionism. Participants learn advanced educational frameworks, curriculum delivery, strategic communication, and effective approaches for teaching, training, and building informed communities.

Our Vision

A world that identifies this era of Jew-hatred and has the courage to confront it.

"A society grounded in historical literacy and moral integrity,"

Dr. Naya Lekht

Contact Us (info@stopaz.org)

At Stop Antizionism, we envision a world in which antizionism is universally recognized as Jew-hatred. We seek a future in which the public understands that the uncritical adulation of decolonization, postcolonial theory, and contemporary human-rights discourse can be, and has been, weaponized to create new hierarchies of virtue and vice, enabling renewed forms of anti-Jewish hatred under the guise of justice.

At Stop Antizionism, we believe that stopping antizionism is inseparable from defending the core values of America and the West. The appearance of antizionism today is part of a wider ideological movement that seeks to delegitimize liberal democracy, erode national cohesion, and dismantle the moral foundations of the free world. Antizionism is not only a danger to Jews; it is a threat to the West itself, its principles of individual dignity, free inquiry, constitutional order, and moral agency.

To confront antizionism is therefore to confront and expose the broader anti-American and anti-Western currents that undermine reason, freedom, and the principles that sustain open societies.

We imagine a society in which individuals possess the intellectual literacy to recognize antizionism’s narrative structure:

  • How it cloaks itself in the language of liberation;
  • How it constructs villains, victims, and rescuers;
  • How it redefines justice through a moral inversion that demonizes Jews and the Jewish state;
  • How it reshapes academic, cultural, and civic life by presenting its ideological claims as unquestionable moral truths.
  • How antizionism functions as a threat to democracy and freedom, eroding the liberal norms and civic trust on which Western societies depend.

In our vision, students, educators, journalists, policymakers, and the public understand that the rise of antizionism is not merely political, but moral and civilizational, a shift in the categories through which societies define good and evil, oppressor and oppressed, humanity and inhumanity.

They recognize that antizionist libels are not debates to be corrected with facts, but tools of demonology that construct the Jew as the violator of the era’s highest virtues.They also recognize that these libels corrode the Western moral imagination itself, replacing universal principles with ideological purity tests that delegitimize entire peoples, nations, and histories.

Stop Antizionism strives for a world capable of identifying this third era of Jew-hatred while it is still unfolding, and courageous enough to confront it.

We work toward a future in which the mechanisms that cast Israel and Zionism as villains are transparent; in which the so-called “rescuer” role is understood as a performative justification rather than a marker of moral virtue; and in which Jews, individually and collectively, are no longer compelled to defend their legitimacy against a narrative engineered for their erasure.

Our vision is a public discourse rooted in historical literacy, moral clarity, and intellectual honesty.

  • A cultural environment where hate movements can no longer disguise themselves as humanitarian projects.
  • And an educational landscape where the Jewish story—its history, indigeneity, and sovereignty—is taught with accuracy, integrity, and depth.

Stop Antizionism works to make this vision a reality by equipping educators, institutions, and communities with the tools to recognize, analyze, and counter the narratives of the antizionist era—transforming scholarship into strategy, and understanding into action.

Naming a civilizational threat clearly and building the courage and capacity to confront it.

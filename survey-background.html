Post-Event Survey Background

The World Symposium Against Antizionism (WSAA) 2026 brought together participants, experts, advocates, and community leaders from around the world to engage in critical discussions on confronting antizionism and contemporary antisemitism. As part of our post-symposium activities, we are conducting a follow-up survey to better understand participants’ experiences and perspectives.

Your feedback is extremely valuable in helping us evaluate what worked well and identify areas where improvements can be made for future symposiums, educational initiatives, and community engagement efforts. The survey covers a range of topics including symposium content, speakers and subject matter experts, logistics, organization, and overall participant experience.

This is a fully anonymous survey. No personally identifying information is collected, and we kindly ask that you refrain from including any identifying details in open-ended responses. If any personally identifying information is inadvertently provided, it will be removed as part of our standard confidentiality practices.

Your participation will help shape and strengthen future programming, priorities, and initiatives.

If you have any questions, please contact us through our website at: info@stopaz.org

Thank you for your time, participation, and valuable feedback.

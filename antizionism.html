Antizionism

STOPAZ confronts antizionism as the third era of Jew-hatred—an old hatred tailored to the moral language of our time.

Analytical framework

Religion. Race. Nation.

The three-era analysis connects antijudaism, antisemitism, and antizionism while distinguishing the specific language and mechanisms of each period.

STOPAZ research examines the history, psychology, propaganda, language, and institutional spread of antizionism.
